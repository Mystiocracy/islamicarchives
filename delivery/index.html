    Format for email delivery

    Kindle deliveries always use EPUB, which Amazon converts for your device.

    Setting up Send to Kindle

    1. On Amazon, open Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings.
    2. Copy your device’s @kindle.com address and add it here.
    3. Add books@islamicarchives.org to the Approved Personal Document E-mail List.
