***Selections of*** **Ahadith *from a forthcoming collection called* Miftah al-Ma\`ani *by Syed Iqbal Zaheer*1. \`A'isha (*ra*) says, "The Messenger was struck by some illness. He began to turn left and right on his bed. I said, 'Messenger of Allah, had one of us be doing this it is feared they would think he is under some spell.' He replied, 'A believer is treated harshly. No believer is struck by an affliction, or disorder, but Allah (*swt*) deletes his sins and raises him by a level.'"**

*-- (Hakim; Dhahabi: 'On conditions of acceptance set by Bukhari and Muslim)*

**2. \`Abdul Rahman b. Azhar reports the Prophet as having said, "The likeness of a bondsman believer, when he is struck by an illness or fever, is like a piece of iron. You place it into the fire and its dirt is removed, leaving the good part."  
**

*-- (Hakim; Dhahabi: 'On conditions of acceptance set by Bukhari and Muslim)*

**3. Abu Hurayrah says the Prophet said, "Allah (*swt*) says: 'When I try a believing slave of Mine, who does not complain to those who visit him, then, I release him from the disease (imposed by me) and exchange his flesh with a better flesh than his and a blood better than his blood. Thereafter,his deeds expatiate (his sins)."**

*-- (Hakim; Dhahabi: 'On conditions of acceptance set by Bukhari and Muslim)*

**4. \`Ali (*ra*) reported the Prophet, "Supplication is a weapon of a believer. It is a pillar of Islam, Light of the heaven and earth."**

*-- Hakim*

**5. Abu Hurayrah says the Messenger of Allah said: 'What I am afraid of my *Ummah* is that wealth might become abundant among them leading to they killing each other over it. And what I fear of the *Ummah* is that the Qur'an will be opened, until a believer will read it, so too will the unbeliever and the hypocrite. Consequently, the believer will declare its lawful as lawful, seeking its truthful explanation, until the last verse."**

*-- (Hakim; Dhahabi: 'It meets the conditions of acceptance set by Bukhari and Muslim)*

**6. On Abu Hurayra's authority, the Prophet said, "When a believer commits a sin it results in a black dot in his heart. If he repents, gives up, and seeks forgiveness, it is removed from his heart. But if he goes on, the dots go on until they cling to the heart. This is the *Ra'n* that Allah (*swt*) spoke of (83: 14): *No indeed! But what they used to earn (of the evil deeds) has cast a layer of rust over their hearts*."  
**

*-- (Hakim; Dhahabi: 'On conditions of acceptance set by Muslim)*

**7. Abu Hurayrah narrated that the Messenger of Allah said, "A believer will never kill anyone unaware, after he had given him assurance of safety. Faith prevents killing someone unaware, after he was given assurance of safety."**

*-- (Hakim; Dhahabi: 'On conditions of acceptance set by Muslim)*
