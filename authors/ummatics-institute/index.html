Ummatics Institute

United States · Research institute, publisher, non-profit

The Ummatics Institute is a research organisation based in the United States and registered as a non-profit. Its founder and chief research officer is Ovamir Anjum. It works for what it calls a unified Islamic civilisation for the benefit of the Umma and humanity, and coins the term ummatics for the study of Muslim solidarity and political unity.

It publishes research papers, reflective articles, recommendation papers, guest contributions and translations, as well as the Hadara Series and annual reports, and holds regular colloquia, meetups and an annual conference. A series of classical texts surveys the juristic schools’ views on the imamate. Its work is funded by individual donations; it states that it takes no government money.

This page lists Ummatics’ written work by contributors who do not have their own author page here; each piece names its writer. Pieces by Ovamir Anjum, Ṭāhā ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, Muzaffar Iqbal and Omar Suleiman are listed on their own pages. Videos, colloquium recordings and calls for papers are not included.
