Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim

The collection of Muslim b. al-Ḥajjāj al-Naysābūrī (d. 261 AH): 7,212 ḥadīth in 56 books, in Arabic and in Abdul Hamid Siddiqui's English translation, numbered as on sunnah.com. The Introduction (muqaddima) is not included. Numbering follows sunnah.com (8a, 8b … share the number 8), so it does not always run in order. Every chain of narrators links to the narrator tree.
