How this list is made

A work appears here when it is filed under Qur'anic studies in the library and its title, original title, summary or keywords name it as a tafsīr, commentary, exegesis or translation of the Qur'an, or when it is catalogued as a translation. A commentary filed under another subject, or described in other words, may be missing; the complete subject list is under Works on the Qur'an, and every work can be found from the library.
