Subjects

Sufism

Mysticism, spirituality and the Sufi orders.

Authors come to Sufism from several directions. Traditionalist writers such as Martin Lings, Frithjof Schuon and Seyyed Hossein Nasr present it as the inner dimension of Islam. Hamid Algar and Nuh Ha Mim Keller describe it as a path anchored in law and practice, and Fethullah Gülen presents its teaching to a modern audience.

Reformist and Salafi writers criticise parts of Sufi practice, and their work is here as well.

  3. 1981 · Book
    Sufism: Veil and Quintessence ↗ Frithjof Schuon

    Schuon's account of Sufism as the quintessence of Islam.

  4. 1998 · Pamphlet
    Sufism: Principles and Practice ↗ Hamid Algar

    Algar on the principles and the discipline of the Sufi path.

  5. 2002 · Pamphlet
    Sufism and Islam ↗ Nuh Ha Mim Keller

    Keller on the place of Sufism within the Sunni tradition.

  6. 2007 · Book
    The Garden of Truth: The Vision and Promise of Sufism, Islam's Mystical Tradition ↗ Seyyed Hossein Nasr

    A popular statement of the vision and promise of Sufism.
