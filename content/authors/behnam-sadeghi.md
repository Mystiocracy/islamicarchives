Behnam Sadeghi is a scholar of Qur'anic studies, Islamic law and early Islamic history who received his PhD from Princeton University in 2006. He was an assistant professor of religious studies at Stanford University from 2006 to 2016. His research includes the early textual history of the Qur'an, hadith, and legal debates about women. With Uwe Bergmann he studied the Ṣanʿāʾ palimpsest, and with Mohsen Goudarzi he produced an edition of its lower text, which is the only known witness to a non-ʿUthmānic text type.
