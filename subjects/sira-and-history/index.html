Subjects

Sira and history

The life of the Prophet, Islamic history, biography, memoir and travel writing.

Biographies of the Prophet are the biggest part of this section, written for different audiences: Shibli Nomani's Sirat al-Nabi (completed after his death by Sulayman Nadwi), Martin Lings's Muhammad, Hamidullah's edition of Ibn Ishaq, and Nasr's Muhammad: Man of Allah. Then come histories of Muslim societies, and memoir and travel writing such as Shibli's travelogue of Istanbul, Cairo and Syria.

Where to begin

A reading order, earliest first. Each title opens the text or the free copy.

  1. 1918 · Book
    Sirat al-Nabi ↗ Shiblī Nuʿmānī

    The Indian scholar's critical biography, finished after his death by his student Sulayman Nadwi.

  2. 1976 · Book
    Sirat Ibn Ishaq Muhammad Hamidullah

    Hamidullah's edition of Ibn Ishaq's early biography of the Prophet.

  3. 1976 · Translation
    The Life of Muhammad ↗ Ismāʿīl al-Fārūqī

    An English version of Haykal's biography, translated by Ismail al-Faruqi.

  4. 1983 · Book
    Muhammad: His Life Based on the Earliest Sources ↗ Martin Lings

    A narrative life of the Prophet drawn from the earliest sources.

  5. 1982 · Book
    Muhammad: Man of Allah ↗ Seyyed Hossein Nasr

    Nasr's short account of the Prophet's life and of his place in Muslim devotion.
