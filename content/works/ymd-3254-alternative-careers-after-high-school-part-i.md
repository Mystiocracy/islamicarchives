***Given the fact that in our country the pass percentage of students in 10th & 12th std exams averages at 55 -- 60 % and in this, at least 50% secure marks between 45 to 60%, the dilemma is what's the best option for such students? A regular two year course at college or get hands on training in two years and become employable? It's a catch-22 situation for many students and parents. But a decision must be taken; else the future is at stake. With this scene in the background, there are the alternative career options in Air ticketing, jewellery designing and fire technology which promise exciting opportunities to the young generation, writes AMEEN-E-MUDASSIR.***  
**IATA COURSES -- AIR TICKETING**

**IATA / UFTAA Level-1: Foundation Course**

This is a 13 module foundation Level course offered by International Air Transport Association (IATA) which provides an understanding of the travel and tourism industry, the practicalities of the travel professional's role in this industry, the importance of exceeding customer expectations, and the role of technology in the travel industry.

Course title: Diploma in Travel and Tourism Foundations.  
Eligibility: 10th standard pass or Diploma.  
Skills required: Good at Maths and English-language skills.

**What do you study in an IATA Course?**

The Business Environment of the Travel and Tourism Industry, Geography in Travel Planning, Travel Formalities, Land & Water transport Ferry, Tour Packages, Air Transport Essentials, Technology in the Travel Industry, Air Fares and Ticketing, Electronic Booking Tools, Global Distribution Systems.

**Benefits of IATA Foundation Course**

After completing the IATA Foundation Diploma Course, the students can make appropriate travel arrangements and reservations, calculate and quote appropriate air fares, Complete international travel documents in accordance with the applicable IATA rules and procedures

**IATA Registration Fees**

Total fees: Rs.21,000/- (Note: The course fees is inclusive of course material, exam fees (two attempts), postal fees, bank transfer and processing cost).

Institutes: To get list of recognized institutes, visit www.iata.org

**Job Opportunities after IATA/UTFA Courses**

1\. Work with travel agencies.  
2. Job opportunities in Airports across the world in ticketing, passenger handling, cargo handling, ground staff, staff, etc..  
3. With Advanced certifications, opportunities are still plenty.

**GEMOLOGY & JEWELLERY DESIGNING**

Gemology is the study of gemstones. Some dictionaries define it as the "scientific study of gemstones". A gemologist is someone who studies gemstones. Today jewelry designing is one of the most sought careers by creative and artistic people.

**Courses Offered**

1\. Diploma in Gemology and Diamond Grading -- five months  
Eligibility : Pass in 10th standard.

2\. Diploma in Coloured gems and diamond grading.  
Eligibility : Pass in 10th standard.

3\. Diploma in Jewelery Designing  
Eligibility: Pass in 12th standard.

4\. Correspondence Courses in Gemmology  
Eligibility: 10th pass. Duration: one year.

**Institutes offering Gemmology Courses:**

**FIRE TECHNOLOGY**

The ever growing industrialization, the unhindered population growth massive urbanization etc.. have all increased the fire risk to a great extent. This has lead to a great demand for trained personnel.

**Great Opportunities in the Gulf**

Gulf is the biggest fire prone area in the world, because it has the largest storage of oil. Hence there are thousands of job openings in the field of Safety as Safety Engineers, Officers and supervisors with starting salaries of Rs.25,000/- per month.
