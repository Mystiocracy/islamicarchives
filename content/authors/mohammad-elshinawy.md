Mohammad Elshinawy is an American Muslim teacher and writer. He earned a bachelor's degree in Islamic studies through Mishkah University and a separate bachelor's in English literature from Brooklyn College in New York.

He serves as religious director at the IECPA in Allentown, Pennsylvania, and teaches for several Islamic education programmes, including AlMaghrib Institute, Mishkah University and Legacy International Online High School. Much of his output has been translation: he has rendered dozens of books and research papers from Arabic into English.

At Yaqeen Institute he has contributed a large body of papers, sermons, video series and Qur'an reflections, with particular attention to prophethood, the Qur'an and the divine names. He is the author of *The Final Prophet: Proof for the Prophethood of Muhammad* and co-author of *When the Stars Prostrated: Meditations on Surat Yusuf* and *The Prophet of Mercy*.
