Shoaib Ahmed Malik is a Pakistani Muslim theologian who lectures in science and religion at the University of Edinburgh. He trained first as an engineer, holding a PhD in chemical engineering from the University of Nottingham, and later took an MSc in science and religion and a second doctorate, in theology, at St Mary's University, Twickenham. He is Sunni and Ashʿarī in creed and has been strongly influenced by al-Ghazālī.

His work centres on where Islamic theology meets the natural sciences. He wrote *Atheism and Islam: A Contemporary Discourse* (2018) and *Islam and Evolution: Al-Ghazālī and the Modern Evolutionary Paradigm* (2021), and has edited volumes on Islamic theology and extraterrestrial life, on design discourse in the Abrahamic traditions, and on Islam and evolution (all 2024). He is chief editor of Palgrave Macmillan's *Islam and Science* book series, sits on the editorial board of the journal *Theology and Science*, and serves on the executive committee of the International Society for Science and Religion.
