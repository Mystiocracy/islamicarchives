Khalid Saifullah Rahmani

خالد سیف اللہ رحمانی

b. 1956 · India (Hyderabad) · Hanafi jurist, mufti, president of the All India Muslim Personal Law Board

Khalid Saifullah Rahmani was born in Bihar, India, in 1956, and is the nephew of the jurist Mujahidul Islam Qasmi. He studied at Jamia Rahmani in Munger, completed the hadith course at Darul Uloom Deoband, and then trained in Islamic jurisprudence and the writing of legal opinions in Patna. He began teaching at Madrasa Sabilus Salam in Hyderabad and later founded the Al-Mahad al-Aali al-Islami, a postgraduate institute of fiqh in that city.

He is general secretary of the Islamic Fiqh Academy of India, edits the juristic journal Bahs-o-Nazar, which his uncle founded, and writes a weekly column in the Urdu press. Within the All India Muslim Personal Law Board he served as general secretary from 2021 and became its president in 2023, speaking for the board on questions such as personal law and waqf legislation.

His books are mostly in Urdu, with some in Arabic and a few English translations. They include Qamus ul-Fiqh, a dictionary of legal terms, the multi-volume Jadid Fiqhi Masail on contemporary questions such as banking, medicine and trade, and shorter works on zakat, women’s rights, principles of fiqh and the Prophet’s biography.
