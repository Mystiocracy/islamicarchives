Reading the tree

  • Colours show the generation (ṭabaqa) in Ibn Ḥajar's scheme in Taqrīb al-Tahdhīb: 1 for the Companions, 2 to 6 for the Successors from senior to junior, 7 to 9 for their followers, and 10 to 12 for the generations Muslim studied with.
  • The number after a name is how many of Muslim's ḥadīth pass along that link. Thicker lines carry more ḥadīth. Tap the name and the card lists those ḥadīth; tap one of them to light up its full chain in gold.
  • A dashed ring means the name has a further generation to open. Long lists open twelve at a time; tap “more” for the rest.
  • Dates are death years in hijri; the narrator card adds the Common Era year.
  • Grades follow muslimscholars.info's summary of Ibn Ḥajar's verdicts: thiqa (trustworthy), thiqa thiqa (doubly so), ṣadūq (truthful), maqbūl (acceptable when supported) and so on.

About the data

The chains come from the Hadith Narrators dataset, which gives the narrators of each ḥadīth in the Six Books as numbered entries from muslimscholars.info. For Muslim it gives chains for 7,032 of the 7,212 ḥadīth here (the Introduction is left out), which were matched to the sunnah.com numbering by comparing their Arabic text. The names, dates, generations and grades were then taken from each narrator's page on muslimscholars.info. A link between two people means one received a ḥadīth from the other in at least one of Muslim's chains.

Some limits follow from this. Muslim often gives one text through several chains, joined by taḥwīl (ح). The dataset lists the names of all of them in one run, so they were separated here by starting a new chain wherever a name comes back; this works for the common patterns, but some secondary chains are cut short and a few may be joined wrongly. Muslim also repeats ḥadīth, and the counts include the repeats. Names in the chains were matched to people automatically, and a few links look doubtful (a Companion shown narrating from a later figure, for instance). Chains that end at a Companion are joined to the Prophet ﷺ; those that end at a later narrator are left as they are. 27 narrators have no generation recorded and 464 have no death date.
