Omar Suleiman is an American Muslim scholar and preacher, born in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1986. His studies span accounting and Islamic law at undergraduate level, master's work in Islamic finance and political history, and a doctorate in Islamic thought and civilisation awarded by the International Islamic University Malaysia in 2020.

Before moving to Texas he served for several years as imam of the Jefferson Muslim Association in the New Orleans area. He later became resident scholar at the Valley Ranch Islamic Center near Dallas and has taught as an adjunct professor of Islamic studies in the Graduate Liberal Studies programme at Southern Methodist University. He was also the founding director of MUHSEN, an organisation supporting Muslims with special needs, and has been active in interfaith work through Faith Forward Dallas.

In 2016 he founded the Yaqeen Institute for Islamic Research, which he leads as president. He is widely known for his lectures, sermons and video series, and his books include *Prayers of the Pious*, *Allah Loves*, *40 on Justice: The Prophetic Voice on Social Reform*, *Angels in Your Presence* and *Meeting Muhammad*.
