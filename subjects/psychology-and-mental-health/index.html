Subjects

Psychology and mental health

Islamic perspectives on the mind, emotions, mental health and wellbeing.

Most of the entries are papers on mental health from the Yaqeen Institute, dealing with anxiety, shame, doubt and trauma. The older authors write about the soul: Hamka's Tasawuf Modern, Muhammad Qutb on the human psyche, al-Attas on the nature of man and Laleh Bakhtiar's handbook of spiritual chivalry.

Where to begin

A reading order, earliest first. Each title opens the text or the free copy.

  1. 1939 · Book
    Tasawuf Modern ↗ Hamka

    Hamka's widely read book on happiness and the spiritual life.

  2. 1963 · Book
    Dirāsāt fī al-nafs al-insāniyya ↗ Muḥammad Quṭb

    Muhammad Qutb on the human psyche from an Islamic point of view.

  3. 1965 · Book
    Tazkiyah-i Nafs ↗ Amin Ahsan Islahi

    Islahi on purifying the self.

  4. 1990 · Essay
    The Nature of Man and the Psychology of the Human Soul ↗ Syed Muhammad Naquib al-Attas

    Al-Attas on the nature of the human soul.

  5. 1994 · Book
    Moral Healer's Handbook: The Psychology of Spiritual Chivalry ↗ Laleh Bakhtiar

    A handbook of moral psychology drawn from the Sufi tradition of futuwwa.

  6. 2017 · Paper
    Islamic Spirituality and Mental Well-Being ↗ Zohair Abdul-Rahman · Yaqeen Institute for Islamic Research

    A Yaqeen paper on spirituality and wellbeing.

  7. 2017 · Paper
    Clinicians, Imams, and the Whisperings of Satan ↗ Najwa Awad · Yaqeen Institute for Islamic Research

    On how clinicians and imams can work together on cases of intrusive thoughts.
