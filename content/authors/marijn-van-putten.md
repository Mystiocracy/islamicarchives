Marijn van Putten is a Dutch historical linguist who studied at Leiden University and received his PhD there in 2013 with a grammar of Awjila Berber. He is an assistant professor at Leiden and principal investigator of the ERC project Qurcan on the canonization of the Qur'anic reading traditions. His research combines Arabic historical linguistics, textual criticism and manuscript evidence to study the Qur'an's early orthography and the development of its reading traditions.
