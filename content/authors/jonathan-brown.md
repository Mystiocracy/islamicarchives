Jonathan A. C. Brown is a historian of Islamic law and hadith at Georgetown University. His books include *The Canonization of al-Bukhārī and Muslim* (2007), *Hadith: Muhammad’s Legacy in the Medieval and Modern World* (2009), *Misquoting Muhammad* (2014), *Slavery and Islam* (2019) and *Islam and Blackness* (2022).
