Amir Hussain

b. 1965 · United States · Scholar of religious studies, professor at Loyola Marymount University

Amir Hussain is a scholar of religion who was born in Lahore and grew up in Toronto. He studied the academic study of religion at the University of Toronto, where his doctoral research, completed in 2001, examined Muslim communities in Toronto. He taught at California State University, Northridge, before moving in 2005 to Loyola Marymount University in Los Angeles, where he is professor of theological studies.

His academic service has included editing the Journal of the American Academy of Religion (2011-2015), serving as president of the American Academy of Religion in 2023, and acting as editor-in-chief of the Oxford Encyclopedia of Islam in North America. He has also been a senior editor for religion at Oxford Handbooks Online and co-edited a widely used world-religions textbook.

His own books are written for general readers and students rather than specialists. Oil and Water (2006) is an introduction to Muslim-Christian relations and the shared Abrahamic inheritance, and Muslims and the Making of America (2016) surveys the long Muslim presence in North America, from enslaved Africans to recent immigrant communities. A later book on Christians and Muslims as neighbours appeared in 2025.
