Slavery and race

Slavery, emancipation and race in Islamic history and law.

This section follows how Muslim scholars have dealt with slavery in law and history and with race and anti-Blackness in the community. Jonathan Brown's Slavery and Islam and Islam and Blackness are the main books. Kecia Ali looks at the legal link between marriage and slavery, and Sherman Jackson writes about Black Muslims in America and the problem of Black suffering.

Yaqeen Institute papers cover Black Muslim history in the United States, from Malcolm X to chaplaincy, and the role of freed slaves (mawālī) in early Islam.

    Islam and Blackness ↗ Jonathan A. C. Brown
