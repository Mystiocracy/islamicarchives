The ultimate goal of the 9/11 perpetrators, many believe, was to generate popular support for wars in various Muslim-domiated countries, with the aim of ensuring U.S. control of, perhaps, the most geopolitically important region in the world.

Gathering and studying the physical evidence is the crucial first step towards understanding how the WTC attacks of 11^(th) September, 2001, occurred. The fact that the World Trade Center crime scene was disturbed --- with tell-tale evidence hastily removed and destroyed --- has created unique challenges for 9/11 investigators.

Fortunately, additional evidence, including definitive seismic data, hundreds of eyewitness accounts of explosions, the documentation of thermite and nano-thermite incendiaries in the WTC debris, and photographic evidence of molten steel/iron in the towers, in the rubble, and in the WTC dust, fill the gap.

All of this impeccable evidence points to the theory of controlled demolition using explosives and incendiaries --- a theory that categorically disproves the official story.

But who could gain entry to plant explosives in highly secure buildings like the Twin Towers and World Trade Center Building 7 (WTC 7)? That question must be answered in a real criminal investigation that has subpoena power and the legal authority to offer immunity.

Just as in any criminal matter, the suspected perpetrators should be charged, tried, and convicted in a court of law before a valid judgment can be rendered as to their guilt or innocence.

The technical experts at AE911 Truth have performed the important task of gathering and analyzing the scientific evidence that proves, beyond a reasonable doubt, that the Twin Towers and WTC 7 were brought down by explosives.

Now that the crime of explosive destruction has been confirmed by our experts, it is the duty of government officials to heed international calls for a real investigation. Until such an inquiry is carried out, the effort to determine the identities of the criminals is limited to those who are willing to conduct the research and construct working theories based on that research.

Whoever destroyed the WTC skyscrapers must have had access to the advanced nano-thermite material that was discovered in the dust samples from Ground Zero.

Many in the 9/11 Truth Movement are convinced that the event was a false-flag operation*,* manufactured by a group composed, at least in part, of elements within the U.S. government. This cabal deflected attention from themselves by holding Middle Eastern Muslims responsible for the catastrophic destruction, stoking the public’s fear of these ‘Islamic terrorists’ and turning popular opinion against them.

The ultimate goal of the 9/11 perpetrators, many believe, was to generate popular support for wars in various Muslim-dominated countries, with the aim of ensuring U.S. control of perhaps the most geopolitically important region in the world.

That agenda required cracking down on public dissent by enacting draconian legislation --- the Patriot Act, for example --- and issuing executive orders (think of the 2012 National Defense Authorization Act). Both acts were designed to curtail civil liberties and to vastly increase the U.S. defense budget.

Indeed, some maintain that the events of 9/11 could have been perpetrated only by factions within the U.S. government and in the upper echelons of major international corporations, especially in defense-related industries.
