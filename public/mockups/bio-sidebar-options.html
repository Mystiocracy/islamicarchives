Biography sidebar: five ways to fix it

Each phone below is 360px wide and scrolls on its own. The text is sample copy, and the styling copies your site's cream, green and serif look so the options are easy to compare. Tap the Contents rows and the bar in option D to try them.

Recommended: B on phones, the same card as a sticky left column on desktop (shown at the bottom), plus the E rule: hide the contents list when a biography has fewer than 4 sections.
Now

Current layout

  1. The two-column layout doesn't stack, so the article is clipped to a sliver.
  2. The title, badge and tabs run off the right edge.
  3. The reader button wraps to two lines and sits apart from the contents card.
  4. The card has a heavy top border and faint borders on the other sides.
  5. Connections, Works and See also repeat the tabs.
A

Stack and collapse

The sidebar moves above the article. The reader button fits on one line and the contents list is collapsed by default.

Effort
CSS media query plus a <details> tag
Trade-off
Two separate elements still push the article down
B

One combined card

The contents list and the reader link share one card with even borders. Links that repeat the tabs are removed.

Effort
CSS plus a small template change
Trade-off
The reader link is less prominent than a button
C

Reader in the header

"Read in reader" becomes a small pill beside the period badge. The contents list shrinks to one line of links.

Effort
Template change, no JavaScript
Trade-off
A one-line list gets long when a biography has many sections
D

Sticky "On this page" bar

A thin bar stays pinned under the tabs and shows the section you are reading. Tap it to jump to another section. The book icon opens the reader.

Effort
About 30 lines of JavaScript for tracking the current section
Trade-off
Most work; pays off on long biographies
E

No contents on short pages

With only two sections, the list is skipped. One quiet row keeps the reader link and the article starts right away.

Effort
One condition in the template: count headings ≥ 4
Trade-off
Works as a rule alongside A–D, not as a design on its own
B

Recommended card on desktop

The same card becomes a sticky left column. Scroll inside the frame; the card stays in view and highlights the current section. On narrow screens, scroll the frame sideways.
