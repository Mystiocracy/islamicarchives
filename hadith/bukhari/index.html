Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī

The collection of Muḥammad b. Ismāʿīl al-Bukhārī (d. 256 AH): 7,580 ḥadīth in 97 books, in Arabic and in Muhammad Muhsin Khan's English translation. Each ḥadīth has its own page with its chain of narrators, and every name in a chain opens that narrator in the narrator tree.
