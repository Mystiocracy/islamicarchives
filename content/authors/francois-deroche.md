François Déroche is a French scholar of Arabic codicology and palaeography who trained in Semitic languages at the École normale supérieure. He catalogued Qur'anic manuscripts at the Bibliothèque nationale, worked at the Institut français d'études anatoliennes in Istanbul, and from 1990 taught the history of the Arabic manuscript book at the École Pratique des Hautes Études. Since 2015 he has held the chair in the History of the Qur'an: Text and Transmission at the Collège de France and he is a member of the Académie des inscriptions et belles-lettres. His research uses the physical features of early parchment codices to reconstruct how the Qur'anic text was written down and circulated.
