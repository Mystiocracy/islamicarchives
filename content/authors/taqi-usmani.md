Muhammad Taqi Usmani was born in Deoband, India, in 1943 and moved to Karachi with his family after Partition. His father was Mufti Muhammad Shafiʿ, the Grand Mufti of Pakistan, and he studied at Dar al-ʿUlum Karachi, which his father founded. He later took degrees at the University of the Punjab and at Karachi, and he is now vice-president of Dar al-ʿUlum Karachi.

He is best known as a leading Deobandi scholar of fiqh, hadith and Islamic finance. He served as a judge of the Shariʿat Appellate Bench of the Supreme Court of Pakistan from 1982 to 2002 and sits on the Sharia boards of many Islamic financial institutions. He chairs the Sharia Council of AAOIFI, and *An Introduction to Islamic Finance* is among his most widely read English works. His Urdu works include a commentary on Sahih Muslim, *Takmila Fath al-Mulhim*, a travelogue and many collections of talks and essays. His Urdu Qurʾan translation and commentary, *Āsān Tarjuma-e-Qurʾān*, was followed by *Tafsīr-e-Tawḍīḥ al-Qurʾān*. His English translation, *The Noble Qur'an: A New Rendering of its Meaning in English*, was made as an English version of his Urdu translation.

Most of his writings remain in copyright; the archive links to the free copies that he, his publishers and his institutions make available.
