Subjects

Family and society

Marriage, parenting, community and Muslim social life.

Family law is covered under Islamic law and gender under women and gender. This section holds writing on community life: upbringing, marriage guidance, memoirs and the shape of a Muslim society. Malek Bennabi's Birth of a Society asks what makes a society from the inside. Al-Tahtawi's Faithful Guide for Girls and Boys is a nineteenth-century Egyptian primer, and Sayyid Qutb's A Child from the Village is a memoir of childhood in an Egyptian village.

Later entries are mostly fatwa collections and guidance literature, from Ali Gomaa and Yusuf al-Qaradawi to the Yaqeen Institute.
