Science and religion

Islam and the natural sciences, technology, evolution and the environment.

Early authors such as al-Afghani, Abduh and Sayyid Ahmad Khan argued over whether Islam is compatible with modern science and how far the Qur'an should be read in its light. From the 1960s Seyyed Hossein Nasr and others turned the question round and asked what modern science leaves out.

Today the debate is largely about evolution (Shoaib Ahmed Malik, Yasir Qadhi, Yaqeen Institute), the environment and, more recently, artificial intelligence.
