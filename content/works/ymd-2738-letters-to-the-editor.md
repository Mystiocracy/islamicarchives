S. Mohammad Thousif, Hyderabad

Please tell me about the authenticity of this picture of Prophet Muhammad (*SAS*) which is in circulation.

**YMD**

We do not know of any picture of the Prophet which is in circulation. It is of course unlawful and if you receive one, you should tear it off without a second's delay. Do not even look into how it is made. Just tear it off.

Somebody told me that graves should not be made *Pucca.* To what extent is this true? Please give a detailed answer according to the *Sahih Hadith.*

**YMD**

There are several trustworthy ahadith that prohibit that graves be plastered or raised high above the ground.
