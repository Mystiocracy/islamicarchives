Osman Umarji

United States · Educational psychologist, adjunct professor, researcher

Osman Umarji is an American researcher in educational psychology. He first qualified in electrical engineering, earning a bachelor’s of science, and went on to complete a master’s degree and a PhD in educational psychology at the University of California, Irvine. His Islamic studies were undertaken at al-Azhar University in Cairo.

He has taught as an adjunct professor in the School of Education at UC Irvine, offering courses in child and adolescent development and in statistics. His research interests cover the development of human motivation, religious socialisation, spirituality and Islamic legal theory.

At Yaqeen Institute he applies empirical methods from psychology to questions facing contemporary Muslims, contributing research papers, essays and video series.
