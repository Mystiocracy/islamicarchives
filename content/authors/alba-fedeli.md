Alba Fedeli is a philologist who studied Classical Philology at the Università Cattolica in Milan and completed a PhD at the University of Birmingham (2011-2015) on early Qur'anic manuscripts and the papers of Alphonse Mingana. Her work covers palaeography, manuscript provenance, phylogenetic analysis and digital editions, including the digitisation of the Mingana-Lewis palimpsest at Cambridge University Library. She has been a research associate at the Asien-Afrika-Institut of the University of Hamburg. She has published on variant readings in the oldest Qur'anic witnesses and on the collecting history of Qur'anic fragments.
