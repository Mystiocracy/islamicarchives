Mohammad Taqi Mesbah-Yazdi

محمدتقی مصباح یزدی

1935–2021 · Iran · Shia philosopher, theologian and teacher in Qom, member of the Assembly of Experts

Mohammad Taqi Mesbah-Yazdi was born in Yazd in 1935 and began his religious education in the seminaries of his home city. After a short stay in Najaf in 1950 he moved to Qom, where he studied jurisprudence and philosophy and completed his seminary training around 1960. His teachers included Hossein Borujerdi, Ruhollah Khomeini and Mohammad Taqi Bahjat, and he is counted among the students of Muhammad Husayn Tabataba’i.

He taught philosophy and theology in Qom for decades and became one of the best-known teachers of Islamic philosophy in the Shia seminaries. He co-founded the Haghani school in Qom with Mohammad Beheshti, directed the Imam Khomeini Education and Research Institute there from its founding in the mid-1990s, and sat in the Assembly of Experts from 1991 to 2016. He was also a prominent conservative voice in Iranian public life.

His writing consists mostly of textbooks and lecture series on philosophy, theology, ethics and political theory. Amuzesh-e Falsafeh, published in English as Philosophical Instructions, became a standard seminary text, and other lectures have appeared in English on divine unity, hadith and ethics.
