Hythem Sidky

United States · Scholar of Qurʾānic manuscripts; Executive Director, International Qurʾānic Studies Association

Hythem Sidky is executive director of the International Qur’anic Studies Association. He trained in chemistry and molecular engineering and was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Chicago before turning his main attention to Qur’anic studies. His work joins philology with statistical and phylogenetic methods to study Qur’anic manuscripts, regional oral traditions and early Arabic inscriptions. He has published on the stemmatics of early codices and on the dotting of the consonantal text.

This scholar is also listed in the Quran Hub’s manuscript scholars catalogue. The list of works here is compiled from publisher, library and university pages and is not exhaustive; check details against the publisher’s record before citing.
