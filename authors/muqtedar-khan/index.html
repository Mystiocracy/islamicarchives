Muqtedar Khan

b. 1966 · United States · Political scientist, professor at the University of Delaware

Muqtedar Khan is a political scientist who works in the United States. He took his doctorate in international relations, political philosophy and Islamic political thought at Georgetown University in 2000. He taught at Adrian College in Michigan before moving to the University of Delaware, where he is a professor of political science and international relations and founded its Islamic studies programme.

He has held a series of fellowships and posts, including a non-resident senior fellowship at the Brookings Institution (2003-2008) and later fellowships at the Institute for Social Policy and Understanding and the New Lines Institute. He has served as an officer of the Association of Muslim Social Scientists and is a frequent commentator on Muslim politics and American foreign policy.

His books deal with American Muslim identity, Islamic political theory and the politics of Jerusalem. They include American Muslims: Bridging Faith and Freedom (2002), Jihad for Jerusalem (2004) and Islam and Good Governance: A Political Philosophy of Ihsan (2019), the last of which proposes an ethic of excellence as a basis for Muslim political thought.
