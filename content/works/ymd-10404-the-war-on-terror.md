It is now eighteen years after the twin towers were brought down in the USA. It is the eighteenth year the American, Israeli, and many other governments and their media have been sobbing over the event. But really? Has it been they? But rather, it is a million Afghan, Iraqi and Syrian women -- who had nothing to do with the event --who have been sobbing for eighteen years. The mission of death, destruction, murder, and rape is accomplished.

But one section, one cadre, one class of humans -- yes, yes, humans, they alone are the humans left on earth -- have been in torment. Mental and psychological torment is no less tortuous than intellectual. It is the intellectual class, the sagacious, the perceptive, the visionary, who are exempt to the hidden 32-teeth smiles. They see no borders. Their concerns are human concerns. They are not church-goers. They cannot be identified by race, religion, region or language. They cannot be identified by their ideology either. While the American, Israeli, and many other governments rejoice at the 9/11 achievement, this class suffers. Suffering? Truly so. "The September 11 attacks were a series of four coordinated terrorist attacks by the Islamic ... 9/11 is the single deadliest terrorist attack in human history and the single deadliest incident for firefighters and law enforcement officers in history..."

So far, the demand has fallen on deaf ears. But, for how long will the ears remain plugged tight?
