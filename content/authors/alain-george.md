Alain Fouad George is a historian of Islamic art and architecture, currently the I.M. Pei Professor of Islamic Art and Architecture at the University of Oxford and a fellow of Wolfson College. He previously taught at the University of Edinburgh and has held a senior role at the Lusail Institute in Qatar. His research covers early Islamic calligraphy, early Qur'an manuscripts, Umayyad religious art and illustrated Arabic books. He is preparing a catalogue of the Al-Sabah Collection's Qur'an manuscripts.
