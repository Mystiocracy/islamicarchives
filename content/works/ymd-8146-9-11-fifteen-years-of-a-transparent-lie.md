These groups of qualified and experienced people say that the official story of 9/11 is false. Architects, engineers, and scientists say that the official story is physically impossible. Firefighters and WTC maintenance personnel say that there were numerous explosions within the towers and that the first explosions were in the sub-basements prior to the buildings being hit by air planes.

Experienced military and civilian pilots say the maneuvers of the aircraft are beyond the capability of the alleged hijackers.
