Tesneem Alkiek is an American scholar of Islamic law. She completed her undergraduate studies in Early Christianity and Islamic Studies at the University of Michigan and then earned a PhD in Islamic Studies at Georgetown University, concentrating on Islamic law.

She teaches as a lecturer in the Department of Philosophy and Religion at Rutgers University-Camden.

At Yaqeen Institute she is Director of Expanded Learning, leading a team that turns the Institute's research into curricula and other materials for use in communities. She has written papers on subjects including marriage and gender roles in Islam, and appears in several Yaqeen video series, among them *Real Talk* and *Quran 30 for 30*.
