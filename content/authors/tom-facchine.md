Tom Facchine is an American imam and writer. He graduated with a BA in political science from Vassar College in 2011 and spent 2015 to 2020 in Madinah, where he earned a BA from the Faculty of Shariah at the Islamic University of Madinah.

He teaches Qur'anic exegesis and Islamic history at Legacy International Online High School.

At Yaqeen Institute he serves as Research Director of Islam and Society. His work concentrates on Islamic political thought and on critical analysis of modern ideologies, and he hosts and appears in a number of the Institute's video programmes.
