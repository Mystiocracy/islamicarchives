Naser Makarem Shirazi was born in Shiraz in 1927. He began formal religious study at about fourteen at a seminary in his home city, moved to Qom in his late teens to attend the classes of Hossein Borujerdi and others, and from 1950 studied in Najaf under major jurists including Muhsin al-Hakim and Abu al-Qasim al-Khoei. According to biographical accounts he was granted ijtihad by senior Najaf scholars while still in his twenties.

Back in Qom from 1951 he taught jurisprudence and legal theory and wrote for the seminary magazine Maktab-e Islam. He was a member of the assembly that drafted Iran's constitution in 1979, later became a marja whose rulings are followed by many Shia, and in 2014 and 2016 organised international conferences against extremism and takfir.

His books range from the multi-volume Persian Qur'an commentary Tafsir Nemuneh to concise works of belief, ethics and law for general readers. Many have been translated into English, and several are widely available through Shia publishing houses.
