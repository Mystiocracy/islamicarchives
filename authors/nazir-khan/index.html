Nazir Khan

Canada · Physician, neuroradiologist, theologian, Senior Fellow at Yaqeen Institute

Nazir Khan is a Canadian physician and Islamic theologian. After medical school he trained in diagnostic radiology at the University of Manitoba and completed a neuroradiology fellowship at the University of Calgary, holding certification in both fields. He has held an assistant professorship at McMaster University and has pursued doctoral research in Islamic theology at the University of Nottingham.

Alongside his medical career he has a traditional religious formation: he memorised the Qur’an in his youth, holds licences (ijāzāt) in all ten canonical readings of the Qur’an, and has received authorisations in the six major hadith collections and a range of theological texts. He has served for many years as a volunteer imam and has advised the fiqh committee of the Manitoba Islamic Association.

His writing addresses questions of faith, reason and science, including atheism and scepticism, Ibn Taymiyyah’s epistemology, the compilation of the Qur’an, and social justice. He is a Senior Fellow at Yaqeen Institute and was the founding president of its Canadian branch.
