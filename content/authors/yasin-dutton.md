Yasin Dutton took his Oxford DPhil in Oriental Studies in 1993 and later taught Arabic and Islamic studies at Edinburgh and Cape Town, where he is now emeritus professor. He is a senior research fellow at Cambridge Muslim College. His research covers the early development of Islamic law, particularly the Madinan school of Malik, and early Qur'an manuscripts and recitation traditions. He has also written on the application of Islamic law to modern economic and environmental questions.
