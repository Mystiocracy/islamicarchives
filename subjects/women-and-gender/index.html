Women and gender

Writing on women, gender and the family.

Writing on women ranges from handbooks on rights and dress to historical studies of how patriarchal readings settled and new readings of the Qur'an itself. Fatema Mernissi looked at gender relations in Muslim societies and at hadith; Amina Wadud and Asma Barlas read scripture against the patriarchal interpretations they had inherited; Kecia Ali and Khaled Abou El Fadl write on the law of marriage and sexual ethics.

Other authors, among them Jamal Badawi and Murtada Mutahhari, set out the case for the traditional structure of rights and duties. The archive includes both lines of argument.
