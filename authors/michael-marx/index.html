Michael Marx

Germany · Islamic studies scholar, Corpus Coranicum

Michael Josef Marx is an Islamic studies scholar attached to the Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities, where he works on the Corpus Coranicum research project. The project assembles manuscript evidence, variant readings and contemporary texts relevant to the Qur’an’s emergence. His published work combines edited volumes on the Qur’anic milieu with studies applying radiocarbon dating to early parchment Qur’an fragments. He also contributes to digital catalogues of Qur’an manuscripts.

This scholar is also listed in the Quran Hub’s manuscript scholars catalogue. The list of works here is compiled from publisher, library and university pages and is not exhaustive; check details against the publisher’s record before citing.
