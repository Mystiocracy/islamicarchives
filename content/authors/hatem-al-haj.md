Hatem al-Haj is a physician and scholar of Islamic jurisprudence based in the United States. He is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics in pediatrics and pediatric hospital medicine and practises as a pediatric hospitalist.

His Islamic legal training includes a master's degree in Islamic law from the American Open University and a PhD in comparative fiqh from al-Jinan University in Tripoli, Lebanon. He has served as dean of the English-language Islamic studies programme at Mishkah University and is a senior member of the standing fatwa committee of the Assembly of Muslim Jurists of America (AMJA).

His contributions to Yaqeen Institute centre on Islamic apologetics and doctrine, including a multi-part series on the principles of defending the faith, the paper *Love and Hate for God's Sake*, and related essays and lectures.
