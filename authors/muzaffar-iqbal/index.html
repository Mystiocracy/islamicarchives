Muzaffar Iqbal

مظفر اقبال

b. 1954 · Canada (born in Pakistan) · Writer on Islam and science, founder of the Center for Islamic Sciences

Muzaffar Iqbal was born in Lahore in 1954 and trained as a chemist, teaching chemistry in Pakistan and Canada before working in Urdu studies, medical research, and scientific administration. In the 1990s he was an official of COMSTECH, the science committee of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, and later of the Pakistan Academy of Sciences.

After returning to Canada in 1999 he directed a science and religion programme at the Center for Theology and Natural Sciences in Berkeley and in 2000 founded the Center for Islam and Science in Alberta, renamed the Center for Islamic Sciences in 2013. He edits a journal on Islamic perspectives on science and civilisation and since 2008 has concentrated on Qur’anic studies, notably as general editor of the Integrated Encyclopedia of the Qur’an.

He has written in both Urdu and English. His English books treat the history of Islamic science and the encounter of Muslims with modern science and technology, while his Urdu works include fiction and a history of the Pakistan movement.
